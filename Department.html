Chemistry Department

Overview of the Department of Chemistry at IIT DHARWAD

The Department of Chemistry at IIT Dharwad is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation in the chemical sciences. Our department brings together a vibrant community of faculty, researchers, and students who are passionate about advancing knowledge and finding solutions to real-world challenges through chemistry. We offer undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs designed to provide students with a strong foundation in both theoretical and experimental aspects of chemistry. With state-of-the-art laboratories, modern instrumentation, and an interdisciplinary approach, we ensure our students gain hands-on experience and the skills required for cutting-edge research and industry applications. Our research spans diverse areas such as organic synthesis, inorganic and organometallic chemistry, physical chemistry, materials chemistry, spectroscopy, catalysis, nanotechnology, and computational chemistry. Faculty members actively collaborate with national and international institutions, fostering a culture of innovation and global engagement. The department also emphasizes student development beyond academics, encouraging participation in seminars, workshops, conferences, and outreach activities. We aim to nurture the next generation of scientists and innovators who will contribute to academia, industry, and society at large..

At IIT Dharwad’s Department of Chemistry, we truly believe: “Where Chemistry Shapes the Future!”

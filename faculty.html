Faculty

Currently our faculty consists of 7 members

Our Department of Chemistry is proud to have a dedicated team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members who are passionate about teaching, research, and innovation. The faculty bring expertise across diverse areas of chemistry including Organic, Inorganic, Physical, Analytical, and Industrial Chemistry.

With a strong commitment to academic excellence, our faculty members not only impart knowledge in classrooms and laboratories but also mentor students in research projects, publications, and career development. Many of them are actively engaged in cutting-edge research, collaborating with national and international institutions, and contributing to advancements in the chemical sciences..

The department encourages a student-centered approach, where faculty members ensure personal guidance, inspire curiosity, and cultivate problem-solving skills. Their collective vision is to create an intellectually stimulating environment that fosters critical thinking, creativity, and innovation in the field of chemistry.
